# Seedance 2.0 Type-Specific Prompt Templates

## 产品展示
Clean commercial flow. Emphasize product clarity, feature demo, result.
Template skeleton:
[Product] is shown in clean modern setting. Camera slowly pushes from medium to close-up. Demonstrate key action with smooth tracking. End on satisfied result shot. Bright even lighting, high detail product commercial style. Soft ambient music + subtle product interaction SFX.

## 生活种草
Natural lifestyle insertion. Soft recommendation feel.
Template skeleton:
In a real everyday scene, [person/product] appears naturally. Soft handheld or gentle tracking. Show seamless integration into daily routine. End with subtle positive reaction. Warm natural light, authentic lifestyle aesthetic. Soft background music, natural ambient sounds.

## 人物口播
Face + speech focused. Lip-sync critical.
Template skeleton:
[Person] faces camera in medium close-up, speaking clearly with natural gestures. Occasional cutaways to supporting visuals. Maintain consistent lighting on face. Dialogue in double quotes for lip-sync. Clean background, professional yet approachable. Clear voice + light background music.

## 搞怪离谱
Absurd comedy. Strong contrast + exaggeration.
Template skeleton:
[Character] starts serious/normal. Suddenly explodes into wild exaggerated actions using [product] in completely wrong ways. Fast cuts, shaky energy, manic expressions. Abruptly returns to deadpan calm and discards item. Chaotic then sudden silence. High-energy absurd comedy, strong visual contrast.

## 剧情反转
Narrative with clear twist point.
Template skeleton:
Establish normal situation and expectation. Build small details that will pay off. At midpoint execute clear reversal. Show new reality and reaction. Keep camera motivated by story beats. Emotional shift must be readable. Soundtrack shifts with the twist.

## 感官氛围 / ASMR
Sound-first, macro, slow rhythm.
Template skeleton:
Extreme close-ups of textures, hands, and micro-actions. Slow deliberate camera moves. Rich layered sound design described in detail (crinkles, soft taps, liquid, fabric). Minimal dialogue. Soft diffused lighting, intimate atmosphere. Prioritize audio immersion over story.

## 伪纪录 / 采访
Observational + interview hybrid.
Template skeleton:
Handheld observational shots mixed with direct-to-camera interview moments. Natural available light, slight imperfections allowed. Subject speaks candidly. Cutaways to real environment details. Documentary framing, authentic tone, minimal music.

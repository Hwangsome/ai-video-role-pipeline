# Full Role Contracts

## Content Strategist
Goal: Produce unambiguous concept_brief.md that locks type and creative direction.
Stop: Brief complete and type field filled.
Handoff: Only concept_brief.md.

## Narrative & Shot Director
Goal: Type-adapted shot_list.md with per-shot duration, camera, emotion, audio, reference needs.
Stop: Shot list complete and totals within 4-15s practical range.
Handoff: shot_list.md + concept_brief.md.

## Visual & Persona Reference Designer
Goal: reference_pack/ (≤9 images) + ref_map.md with clear [ImageN] usage notes.
Stop: Consistency checked for people or product; map accurate.
Handoff: pack + map + upstream files.

## Seedance Multi-Type Prompt Engineer
Goal: seedance_prompts.md ready to paste, with correct tags, type template applied, audio and camera language, parameter suggestions.
Stop: Self-checked against Seedance multimodal syntax.
Handoff: prompts + all upstream.

## Generation & Type-QA Specialist
Goal: Execute generation and produce final video + type-specific qa_report.md.
Stop: QA pass or iteration limit reached.
Handoff: Deliverables to user.
